DIGITAL SERVICE DELIVERY

From first brief
to thoughtful launch.

We deliver custom digital services and software workflows, not physical products. Project milestones are confirmed in writing before work starts.

Our delivery process

  1. Discovery: understand clinic channels, FAQs, scheduling systems, languages and staff workflows.
  2. Proposal: agree deliverables, fees, dependencies, milestones and estimated timelines.
  3. Configuration: prepare approved content and consent-based communication flows.
  4. Testing: review routine scenarios, escalations and operational safeguards with clinic staff.
  5. Deployment and handover: enable agreed features, document operation and explain maintenance responsibilities.

Integrations, business platform approvals, access permissions and customer feedback can affect delivery dates. No fixed implementation timeline is promised before requirements have been reviewed.

Delivery method

Work is delivered remotely through agreed website, messaging, cloud or admin systems. There is no physical shipping for these services.
