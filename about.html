SmileRelay AI is a digital service brand operating from Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India, helping dental clinics explore practical patient communication automation.

Why we exist

Dental teams answer repeat questions while also managing visits, scheduling and ongoing patient care. Our work focuses on the communication layer: answering approved general questions, collecting non-clinical enquiries and helping reception teams review the next step.

We work with clinics to map their workflows and configure the appropriate experience. An illustrative website demo is not a promise that every feature is ready for a clinic without integration.

Human-led by design

Clinical judgment stays with licensed professionals. Appointment confirmations remain with clinic staff unless a tested scheduling integration explicitly supports live availability. WhatsApp messaging requires appropriate business setup, approval and consent.

Work with us

We welcome business enquiries internationally, including Qatar and other markets. The next step is a free discovery conversation about your clinic's communication needs.
