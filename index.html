QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A more thoughtful way
to automate.

We believe good automation is clear about where it helps—and where your people are essential.

Does the assistant book appointments automatically?+

It can collect appointment requests right away. Direct booking is only possible after a compatible scheduling system is integrated, clinic rules are approved and availability is verified. Otherwise reception confirms the time.

Can it work with WhatsApp?+

WhatsApp workflows are possible after suitable WhatsApp Business Platform setup, provider approval, and patient opt-in. Availability, messaging costs and permitted template types depend on Meta and the chosen provider.

Can patients ask medical questions?+

The assistant should not diagnose, prescribe or replace a dentist. We configure it to provide clinic-approved general information and refer symptoms, emergencies and medical decisions to qualified professionals.

Can it support Arabic and English?+

Multilingual flows can be scoped for your clinic, including English and Arabic. Content must be reviewed and tested by your team before launch; language coverage is not assumed by default.

How long does setup take and what does it cost?+

Timing and pricing depend on your channels, integrations, clinic workflow and review needs. We discuss those requirements before proposing a realistic project scope.
