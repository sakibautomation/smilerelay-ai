CANCELLATION & REFUNDS

Transparency
before payment.

Last updated 13 November 2026. Our work is custom-built, so payment and cancellation rules are set out in your written quotation before paid work begins.

Free consultations

Introductory enquiries and free walkthrough requests have no charge, so no refund is needed.

Custom implementation projects

For paid projects, the written agreement defines milestones, deposits and cancellation terms. If you wish to cancel before work begins, contact us as soon as possible. Work already delivered or third-party services already purchased may affect refund eligibility, subject to the agreement and applicable law.

Recurring support

Optional maintenance or support renewals, billing periods and notice requirements are specified in your accepted proposal. Visitors are never automatically subscribed by submitting a demo enquiry.

Requesting a refund or cancellation

Email support@smilerelayai.com with your clinic name, relevant invoice or project reference, payment date and explanation. Do not include complete payment card details. We will review the request against agreed deliverables, applicable law and any provider charges, and communicate our decision in writing.

Approved refunds

Approved refunds are normally returned through the original payment channel where supported. Processing and settlement periods depend on the payment provider and bank; any timeline or exception will be communicated with the decision. Statutory rights are not limited by this policy.
