We design communication tools around the way your dental practice actually works, with clear boundaries for clinical questions and appointment confirmations.

AI website receptionist

Answer approved non-clinical questions, collect general enquiries and offer a route to your reception team. We train and test the experience against the information your clinic provides; it is not a replacement for a dentist.

WhatsApp chatbot & automation

Design flows for routine messaging, enquiry capture, opt-in reminders and staff handoffs. WhatsApp Business Platform setup, provider approvals, consent, template restrictions and usage costs are reviewed before a project goes live.

Appointment requests & reminders

Capture preferred visit dates and contact details and route requests to staff. Live appointment confirmation requires a compatible scheduling integration that has been tested against actual availability. Otherwise reception confirms bookings.

Our project process

  1. Map reception enquiries, policies and channels.
  2. Approve content, privacy safeguards and escalation paths.
  3. Configure and test each patient interaction flow.
  4. Launch the agreed features and refine based on team feedback.
