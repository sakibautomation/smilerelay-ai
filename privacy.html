PRIVACY

Your enquiry.
Thoughtful handling.

Last updated 12 November 2026. This notice describes how information is processed when you contact SmileRelay AI through our website.

Contact details

SmileRelay AI provides AI-powered dental practice communication and workflow services from Kashmir, India. Privacy enquiries can be sent to support@smilerelayai.com.

What we collect and why

When a clinic submits our demo form, we collect the representative's name, business email, phone number, clinic name, country or region, chosen service and optional message. We use these details to respond to enquiries, arrange demonstrations, discuss requested services, and maintain normal business correspondence.

Do not include patient names, symptoms, medical records or sensitive health information. The public form is for business enquiries, not clinical services.

Zoho Forms and email delivery

The public demo request form is embedded from Zoho Forms. When you load or submit it, your browser communicates with Zoho, and submitted information is handled by Zoho Forms and Zoho Mail for notifications. Please review Zoho's privacy notice to learn more about its processing.

Technical information and cookies

Website hosting and form providers may collect technical logs including IP address, browser type and request times to operate their services and prevent abuse. If analytics or advertising services are added later, our notice and any required consent choices will be updated.

Retention and disclosures

We keep business enquiries for as long as reasonably required to respond, operate our business, prevent misuse or meet legal duties. We use external providers for site hosting and email processing; international processing may occur according to their service arrangements. We do not sell enquiry form details to advertisers.

Your requests

You may ask to access, correct or delete your contact details by emailing support@smilerelayai.com. We may verify your request and retain information where required by law. We will update this notice if our services or practices change.
